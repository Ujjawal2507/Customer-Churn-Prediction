Recall Improvement Strategy

Five techniques applied to maximize churn detection at an acceptable precision tradeoff.

1
Lower classification threshold
Default 0.5 → 0.32. Catch more churners without retraining the model.
+5–10% recall gain
2
SMOTE oversampling
Synthetic minority oversampling on training data to balance the 73 / 27 split.
+5–8% recall gain
3
class_weight = 'balanced'
Penalizes misclassifying the minority class more heavily during training.
+5–8% recall gain
4
XGBoost scale_pos_weight
Auto-calculated ratio ≈ 2.77 passed to XGBoost to handle imbalance natively.
+3–6% recall gain
5
GridSearchCV with scoring = 'recall'
Hyperparameter tuning that directly optimizes recall instead of accuracy.
+2–5% recall gain
